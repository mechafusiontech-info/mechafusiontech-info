FAQ

Common questions

How do I get a price?

Send your requirement through the quote builder or by email. We reply with scope, timeline and a quote. Pricing depends on complexity, so we quote per project.

What files will I receive?

CAD work: native files plus PDF, DXF or STEP as needed. Video and photo work: final exports in the format you need.

Can you work from a sketch or an idea?

Yes. A hand sketch, photo or rough description is enough to start. We confirm details before designing.

Do you work with clients outside Vadodara?

Yes. Design, drafting and editing are delivered online anywhere. Physical prototypes can be arranged on request.

Is my project kept confidential?

Yes. We do not publish client work or names without permission.
